Ingredients

Key Ingredients Powering Tenurima™

  • Hawthorn Leaf & Flower

    Hawthorn has been used for centuries to support heart health and healthy circulation. It helps promote proper blood flow and supports overall cardiovascular balance, making it a cornerstone ingredient in Tenurima™.

  • Garlic Bulb

    Garlic is widely known for its ability to support cardiovascular wellness. It helps promote healthy blood vessel function and supports balanced blood pressure levels already within the normal range.

  • Olive Leaf

    Olive leaf is rich in antioxidants that help protect cells from oxidative stress. It supports circulation and helps maintain healthy cardiovascular function as part of a daily wellness routine.

  • Hibiscus Flower

    Hibiscus has been traditionally used to support healthy blood flow and vascular function. It helps promote relaxation of blood vessels, supporting balanced blood pressure and circulation.

  • Vitamin B6 (Pyridoxal-5-Phosphate)

    Vitamin B6 plays a key role in red blood cell formation and oxygen transport. It helps support energy levels and proper circulation throughout the body.

  • Vitamin B12 (Cyanocobalamin)

    Supports red blood cell production and helps maintain healthy nerve function. Adequate B12 levels are essential for proper oxygen delivery and overall cardiovascular wellness.
